# Responsible Data Card

## Dataset Purpose

### Decision this dataset may support

This dataset may support customer churn analysis and help identify customers who may be at risk of leaving a service.

The prediction should be used as decision support for a human customer-support or retention team.

### Decisions this dataset must not make

The model must not automatically:

* Cancel a customer's account
* Deny a customer a service
* Increase a customer's price
* Penalize a customer
* Make other important decisions without human review

---

## Provenance and Permission

The supplied dataset is a customer churn training dataset.

The CSV does not provide information about:

* The original data collector
* How the data was collected
* Whether customers provided consent
* Licensing restrictions
* The exact time period of data collection

Therefore, the provenance and permission status cannot be fully verified from the supplied dataset.

Before real-world use, the organization should verify the source, permission, privacy requirements, and licensing conditions of the data.
